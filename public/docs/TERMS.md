# IGNIS Terms of Use and Limitations

**Last reviewed:** November 2026

IGNIS is provided as a portable research-exploration demonstration. It helps users discover NASA-linked records, inspect documented parameters, compare source descriptions, and experiment with browser-side CSV analysis.

## Permitted use

Users may use the application for research exploration, education, prototyping, and internal evaluation. Users are responsible for the files and questions they submit and for checking important claims against the original source records.

## No operational or safety certification

IGNIS is not a spacecraft fire-risk certification system, emergency-response tool, engineering sign-off system, medical device, or substitute for NASA standards, mission procedures, peer review, calibrated measurements, or professional advice. It does not predict emergencies or produce an operational fire-risk score.

The default charts and CSV are synthetic and illustrative. They are not NASA measurements, a fitted physical model, a validated simulation, or an operational threshold.

## Sources and external services

NASA, NASA Glenn Research Center, NASA Physical Sciences Informatics, and NASA Technical Reports Server remain the source organizations for the linked records. Their content, names, and terms remain theirs. IGNIS does not claim ownership of external source material.

Optional configured AI is an independently managed service selected by the operator. Generated answers may be incomplete or wrong even when they include source links. Users must verify consequential claims against primary sources.

## Availability and changes

The application is supplied without a guarantee of availability, accuracy, fitness for a particular purpose, or uninterrupted operation. Operators are responsible for deployment security, dependency maintenance, access controls, logging, backups, and legal review.

These terms are a project-level demonstration notice, not legal advice. A public deployment should replace or supplement them with terms prepared for the operator’s jurisdiction and service model.
