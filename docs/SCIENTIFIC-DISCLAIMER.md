# IGNIS Scientific Disclaimer

IGNIS distinguishes four different things: NASA-linked source records, publications and overview descriptions, repository metadata, and actual experimental measurements. A source record can establish what an organization says an investigation studies. It does not automatically provide the underlying measurements, calibration, uncertainty budget, or permission to infer a new result.

The local sample CSV and demonstration chart are synthetic and illustrative. They exist to exercise parsing, validation, filtering, charting, and export. They must not be cited as NASA measurements or treated as a physical model.

Comparison output shows documented fields only. Blank or unavailable fields are not estimates. Fire Safety content is qualitative context and does not certify a material, prescribe crew action, calculate a fire-risk score, or establish an emergency threshold.

The optional Assistant is bounded to the indexed source context, but its output still requires human verification. Users should open the original NASA or PSI/NTRS source before relying on a statement in research, engineering, safety, or mission work.
